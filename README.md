## Official & Planning

Essential official portals and immigration requirements for entry and travel within Argentina.

- [Argentina Travel (Argentina.travel)](https://www.argentina.travel/en) - Official tourism website of Argentina featuring destination highlights, entry updates, and itinerary ideas.
- [Argentina Migration & Visas (Migraciones)](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/interior/migraciones) - Official portal for Argentine immigration regulations, reciprocity fee info, and visa categories.
- [National Parks Argentina (APN)](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/parquesnacionales) - Guide to national parks, online ticket bookings, visitor rules, and trail conditions across Argentina.
- [SMN Argentina Weather](https://www.smn.gob.ar/) - Official National Meteorological Service offering forecasts, storm warnings, and regional climate tracking.

## Transportation

Navigating long-haul buses, domestic air routes, and transit systems across the country.

- [Plataforma 10](https://www.plataforma10.com.ar/) - Major booking engine for long-distance bus transportation throughout Argentina and neighboring countries.
- [SUBE Card System](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sube) - Official guide for obtaining and recharging the national public transportation card used in major urban transit systems.
- [Aerolíneas Argentinas](https://www.aerolineas.com.ar/) - National flag carrier providing domestic connections across remote provinces and major cities.
- [Flybondi](https://flybondi.com/) - Low-cost domestic airline operating across major regional routes within Argentina.
- [JetSMART Argentina](https://jetsmart.com/ar/es/) - Ultra-low-cost carrier serving primary and secondary domestic destinations.

## Destinations & Regions

### Buenos Aires

- [Turismo Buenos Aires](https://turismo.buenosaires.gob.ar/en) - Comprehensive visitor guide for the capital, detailing neighborhood circuits, cultural events, and museum schedules.
- [BA Como Llego](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/comollego) - Official route planner for navigating subte (subway), bus, and train routes in Autonomous City of Buenos Aires.

### Patagonia & Fireland

- [El Calafate & Perito Moreno Guide](https://www.elcalafate.tur.ar/) - Visitor guide for exploring the Southern Patagonian Ice Field, glaciers, and local treks.
- [Bariloche Tourism](https://www.barilocheturismo.gob.ar/) - Official guide for the Argentine Lake District, featuring ski resorts, trekking maps, and craft breweries.
- [Ushuaia End of the World](https://turismoushuaia.com/) - Essential travel resource for Tierra del Fuego, Beagle Channel tours, and Antarctic expeditions.

### Wine & North Regions

- [Mendoza Travel](https://mendoza.tur.ar/) - Visitor information for the Cuyo wine region, bodega tours, and high-altitude Andes excursions.
- [Salta Tourism](https://www.turismosalta.gov.ar/) - Official tourist hub for exploring Northern Argentina's high-altitude valleys, salt flats, and cultural heritage routes.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers and remote workers navigating Argentina.

- [Buenos Aires Digital Nomads](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/nomadasdigitales) - Official city initiative details on digital nomad visas, coworking spaces, and community perks.
- [Workation Argentina](https://www.argentina.travel/en/workation) - Government resource guide tailored for combining remote work with regional travel.

## Safety & Cultural Etiquette

Practical advice on currency handling, safety measures, and cultural norms.

- [Argentine Mate Culture](https://rutadelamate.org.ar/) - Cultural guide explaining ritual traditions, proper etiquette, and preparation of Yerba Mate.
